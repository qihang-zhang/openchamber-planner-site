Privacy policy

OpenChamber Planner · effective 10 November 2026

In short

The Planner runs on your own machine, as a service of your OpenChamber. It talks to Google's APIs directly from there. Nothing you allow it to read is sent to the Planner's developers or to anyone else: there is no Planner server, no analytics, no advertising, and nothing is sold.

What it may access, and why

Google asks you which features to allow when you sign in; the Planner asks only for the scopes of the features you choose.

FeatureGoogle scopesWhat the Planner does with them
Who you areopenid, emailKnows which Google account is connected, to show it and to keep several accounts apart.
Calendarcalendar.calendarlist.readonly, calendar.eventsShows your calendars beside the plan; writes, moves and removes the working sessions you schedule, and the events you create or change in the Planner.
TaskstasksShows and updates your Google Tasks lists and tasks as you change them in the Planner.
Gmailgmail.readonlyShows messages so you can turn one into a task, with its subject, sender and a link.
Gmail actionsgmail.modifyOnly if you allow it: archives, trashes, marks read or unread, stars and labels a message you act on from its task. It never deletes permanently.
Drivedrive.metadata.readonlyFinds files by name, to attach a link to a task. It never reads file contents.
Contactscontacts.readonly, contacts.other.readonlySuggests people to invite to an event.

Where your data stays

  • The access Google grants (its refresh token) is stored on your machine, in a file only your user account can read, outside the plan's synced data. It is never shown to the Planner's own web page.
  • What the Planner reads from Google is used to draw your plan and is kept on your machine; it is not sent elsewhere.
  • The Planner's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

In the Planner, Settings → Google → Remove disconnects an account and revokes its access at Google. You can also revoke it at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Removing the Planner's files from your machine removes everything it kept.
