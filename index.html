OpenChamber Planner

A daily planner inside OpenChamber: plan the day, time-box it on your calendar, and keep Linear, GitHub, Google Calendar, Google Tasks and Gmail in one place.

What it does

  • Plans days and weeks in the style of Sunsama: tasks, planned and actual time, channels, rituals.
  • Shows your Google calendars beside the plan and writes the working sessions you schedule to the calendar you choose.
  • Reads and updates Google Tasks, shows Gmail messages you can turn into tasks, and finds Drive files to attach by name.
  • Manages Linear and GitHub issues where they are, and can hand a task to an OpenCode agent.

Signing in with Google

The Planner runs on your own machine, as a service of your OpenChamber. When you choose Sign in with Google, Google asks you which features to allow (Calendar, Tasks, Gmail, Drive, Contacts). The Planner then talks to Google's APIs directly from your machine; the access it receives stays there, in a file only your user account can read. See the privacy policy for exactly what is read, what is written, and why.
